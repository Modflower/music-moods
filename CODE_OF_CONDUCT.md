# Code of Conduct

We believe the best way to have a functional project includes having an inclusive community,
one that respects the project, and provides truthful information to the best of its abilities.

If trans rights, antifascism and disallowing generative AI isn't your fancy, then you're invited to leave.

## `1`. Follow relevant Terms of Services and Community Guidelines.

This should be a given, follow the terms of services and community guidelines of platforms you are on.

## `2`. Respect others around you.

This shall include users of accessibility tooling.

Please provide alt-text descriptions of assets you post, as those who are blind, low vision, deaf, hard of hearing, or
just don't understand the language the asset is in, all benefit from being able to take plain text to have read aloud,
read directly if the asset is difficult to listen to audio,
and translate to their native language for better understanding.

Zero tolerance will be given to any forms of bigotry and harassment, including outside of this project.

Drive by sarcastic, rude, or hostile comments will be removed,
they are not productive and only serves to cause a flame war, and makes the environment hostile.

Bigotry includes but is not limited to:
racism, xenophobia, colorism, featurism, casteism, pluralphobia, transphobia, homophobia, queerphobia, sexism, ableism,
religiophobia (incl. islamophobia, antisemitism, otherwise hatred based on religion or lack thereof),
classism, and fascism.

Please don't reach out to personal accounts of maintainers or collaborators of this project except within this
repository,
and designated public spaces such as [Discord], unless you have been granted permission otherwise.

Do not provide Generative AI any material from this project and its repository,
nor any communications and material made by community members.
Do not use Generative AI to communicate with the project, its repository and the surrounding community.
There is no reasonable expectation for materials and communications to be sent off to a third party,
and for that to respond back as if you said it. If you cannot care to take the time to respond yourself,
we do not want you around the project.

[Discord]: https://discord.gg/EmPS9y9

## `3`. No unwanted content.

This includes but is not limited to: adult content, malicious content, advertising, spamming and illegal content.

This also includes Generative AI content, as defined in [the contributor guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## `4`. Request for English only, please.

We can't moderate every language, nor can communicate in every language.
If you're here for support, we may request you to use your native language, as translators can erase important
information.

## `Ω`. Reservation of rights.

We reserve the right to remove anyone from the project and amend these rules for any reason.
Please do not be the reason we have to update these rules.
